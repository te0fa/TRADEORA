## Section 1 — Certification Statement

The **Tradeora Enterprise AI Architecture Council** hereby formally certifies that the
**Enterprise AI Capability Registry** for the Tradeora Financial Operating System has been
thoroughly reviewed, validated, stress-tested against constitutional mandates, and approved
for **Enterprise Production Deployment**.

This certificate attests that every AI engine operating within or planned for the Tradeora
platform has been inventoried, documented, governed, and made discoverable through the
authoritative registry maintained at `e:\tradeora\docs\AI_CAPABILITY_REGISTRY.md`.

This certification is issued under the authority of:
- **Tradeora Engineering Constitution** (all Articles, with emphasis on Articles 6, 8, 11, 17, 18, 29)
- **Egyptian PDPL 2020** (Personal Data Protection Law)

| Ethics Board meeting | Quarterly | Chief AI Officer (Chair) |

### 6.3 Version Control Commitments

- Every new engine version is registered in `AI_VERSION_HISTORY.md` before production deployment
- Shadow-mode testing minimum: 7 days for MAJOR changes, 3 days for MINOR, 1 day for PATCH
- Canary rollout: 5% → 25% → 100% with 48-hour observation windows
- Every version artifact archived to MinIO WORM (immutable, 7-year retention)
- Rollback capability maintained for 90 days after any version promotion

### 6.4 Continuous Evolution Commitment

The Tradeora AI Capability Registry is a **living document**. As the platform grows:
- Phase 2 (GCC expansion): New engines for Tadawul, DFM/ADX markets, Gulf Arabic NLP, Sharia screening
- Phase 3 (Full MENA): Additional regional intelligence engines
- All new engines MUST be registered before any user-facing exposure
