## Section 1 — AI Safety Philosophy

Financial AI operates in a high-stakes domain where hallucinations, biases, or overconfidence can lead to direct catastrophic financial loss for users and severe regulatory liability for Tradeora. Our philosophy is rooted in extreme caution, verifiable transparency, and strict subordination of AI to human judgment.

### 1.1 The Inviolable AI Principles (From Constitution)
1. **Verifiability Over Elegance:** AI outputs must be mathematically and logically traceable to underlying EGX market data.
2. **Conservative Defaults:** In cases of uncertainty, the AI must default to 'HOLD' or 'INSUFFICIENT_DATA'.
3. **Transparent Uncertainty:** The AI must explicitly state its confidence level and the factors reducing that confidence.
4. **No Predictive Absolutes:** The AI is strictly forbidden from using terms implying certainty or guaranteed returns.
5. **Human Primacy:** The AI is an assistant, not an executor.

### 1.2 The Advisory-Only Guarantee (Article 6)
As mandated by Article 6 of the Tradeora Constitution, the AI engine (WisdomEngine) operates strictly in an **advisory-only** capacity. The system architecture physically prevents the AI from directly submitting orders to the OmsEngine. 
* **Human-in-the-Loop:** A human user must manually review, authorize, and cryptographically sign any trade suggested by the AI.

## Section 2 — The 7 Safety Checks (Detailed)

Every recommendation generated by the WisdomEngine must pass through the `AISafetyEngine`. If any of the following 7 checks fail, the recommendation is immediately suppressed, and a safe fallback message is presented to the user.

### Check 1: Minimum Confidence Gate
* **Threshold:** The consensus confidence score from the MoE (Mixture of Experts) must be `≥ 0.75` (represented as a Decimal).
* **Rationale:** A score below 0.75 indicates severe divergence among the analytical schools, meaning the model is more uncertain than reliable.
* **Action on Failure:** `BLOCK` - Status set to `INSUFFICIENT_CONFIDENCE`.
* **User Message (Arabic):** "التحليل غير كافٍ حالياً، يرجى المراجعة لاحقاً" (Analysis is currently insufficient, please review later).

### Check 2: Minimum School Participation
* **Threshold:** At least `9` out of the `17` analytical schools must have successfully returned a valid sub-analysis.
* **Rationale:** A majority consensus is required. A minority of schools participating indicates missing data for specific analytical dimensions.
* **Action on Failure:** `BLOCK` - Status set to `INSUFFICIENT_SCHOOL_COUNT`.

### Check 3: Market Data Freshness
* **Threshold:** The most recent tick for the instrument in the EGXMarketData BC must be `≤ 900 seconds` (15 minutes) old.
* **Active Session Rule:** If the EGX is currently in an active trading session, this threshold is aggressively tightened to `≤ 60 seconds`.
* **Action on Failure:** `BLOCK` - Suppress recommendation to prevent trading on stale data.

### Check 4: EGX Circuit Breaker Status
* **Check:** Is the specific instrument currently under an active EGX circuit breaker halt?
* **Source:** Evaluated via cross-reference between MarketSchedule BC and EGXMarketData BC.
* **Action on Failure:** `BLOCK` - Providing recommendations during a circuit breaker halt poses severe price manipulation risks.

### Check 5: Instrument Trading Status
* **Check:** Is the instrument's status marked as `SUSPENDED` or `HALTED` by the exchange?
* **Action on Failure:** `BLOCK`.

### Check 6: Arabic Explanation Exists
* **Check:** The generated Arabic rationale string must be non-empty AND contain `≥ 50 words`.
* **Rationale:** Enforces the Arabic-first UX mandate. A user must never receive a BUY/SELL signal without a substantial, culturally appropriate, and detailed explanation.
* **Action on Failure:** `BLOCK`.


### Check 7: FRA Embargo Check (Intraday Real-Time)
* **Check:** The instrument must NOT be present on the active FRA embargo/restricted list.
* **Update Frequency:** Synchronized **every 5 minutes** during EGX trading session (09:00–15:30 Cairo) via `FRAEmbargoSyncJob` (BullMQ). Full daily baseline sync at 00:01 Cairo time. Intraday halts are captured within the 5-minute polling window. See `INTRADAY_FRA_EMBARGO_SYNC_SPECIFICATION.md`.
* **Storage:** Embargo status stored in Valkey SET `fra:embargo:active:{ticker}` with TTL = end of trading day. Check reads from Valkey for sub-millisecond lookup.
* **Fallback:** If FRA API is unreachable for > 10 minutes during session, ALL ticker recommendations are suspended as a precaution until sync resumes.
* **Action on Failure:** `BLOCK` — Strict regulatory requirement.

## Section 3 — Hallucination Detection & Mitigation

In the financial context, an "AI hallucination" is defined as the generation of invented company facts, mathematically incorrect financial ratios, or fabricated news events. 

### 3.1 Detection Methods
1. **Factual Grounding Checks:** Every entity, number, and ratio mentioned in the AI's natural language explanation is parsed (using a specialized extractor LLM) and strictly validated against the internal relational database.
2. **Confidence-Factual Alignment:** If an explanation expresses absolute certainty ("Earnings skyrocketed") but the underlying quantitative data shows marginal growth (+1%), the output is flagged for contradiction.
3. **Cross-School Consistency:** If the overall consensus is BUY, but the value investing school voted SELL due to high P/E, the natural language summary must accurately reflect this dissonance. Contradictions between the numerical votes and the text trigger a block.

### 3.2 Mitigation & Evaluation
* **Target:** Hallucination rate must remain `< 2%` monthly.
* **Golden Dataset:** Evaluated monthly against 100 highly complex, curated EGX scenarios with known ground truth.
* **On Detection:** If a hallucination is detected in real-time by the factual grounding checker, the specific output is BLOCKED, and the system attempts a single regeneration using a highly constrained, fallback prompt.

## Section 4 — Bias Detection & Fairness

Tradeora must ensure equitable analysis across all market sectors and capitalizations.

### 4.1 Tracked Biases
* **Recency Bias:** Over-weighting breaking news at the expense of long-term fundamentals.
* **Sector Bias:** Systematically favoring specific sectors (e.g., Tech/Fintech) over others (e.g., Materials/Industrials).
* **Market Cap Bias:** Asymmetric recommendation frequencies favoring large-cap over mid/small-cap entities.
* **Sentiment Bias:** Over-reacting to highly emotional Arabic financial journalism.

### 4.2 Measurement & Remediation
* **Monthly Audit:** The data science team runs a bias audit comparing the distribution of BUY/SELL recommendations against the actual sector weightings in the EGX 30 index.
* **Fairness Requirement:** No single sector can receive `> 3×` more BUY recommendations than its proportional market index weight over a 30-day rolling window.
* **Remediation Loop:** If bias is detected, the weights assigned to specific schools within the MoE (Mixture of Experts) are algorithmically adjusted to penalize the schools driving the bias.

## Section 5 — AI Incident Response

AI incidents are classified by severity, with strict runbooks for each tier.

| Class | Definition | Immediate Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Class A** | Safety Gate blocked > 50% of all recommendations for > 30 mins | Page On-Call. Pause AI Generation globally. |
| **Class B** | AI accuracy (vs. market movement) drops < 55% over 30 days | Alert Data Science. Initiate model retraining. |
| **Class C** | Hallucination rate exceeds 5% in a single week | Rollback to previous model version via Unleash. |
| **Class D** | AI generated a recommendation violating the FRA embargo | See Playbook below. File regulatory report. |

```python
# Playbook: Class D Incident Response
def handle_class_d_incident(ticker: str, recommendation_id: str):
    """Executes mandatory regulatory response for an FRA embargo violation."""
    
    # 1. Immediately revoke the specific recommendation from all caches and databases
    recommendation_service.revoke(recommendation_id)
    
    # 2. Block all future recommendations for this ticker for 24h at the safety layer
    safety_engine.add_temporary_block(ticker, hours=24)
    
    # 3. Notify any user who viewed the recommendation (Regulatory requirement)
    notification_service.send_regulatory_retraction(recommendation_id)
    
    # 4. File an automated incident report with the internal compliance team
    compliance_service.file_incident(IncidentClass.D, ticker, recommendation_id)
    
    # 5. Alert executive leadership
    pagerduty.trigger_critical_alert('FRA_EMBARGO_VIOLATION', ticker)
```

## Section 6 — Regulatory Compliance for AI

Tradeora's AI operates under two primary regulatory frameworks in Egypt.

### 6.1 FRA (Financial Regulatory Authority) Requirements
* **Non-Custodial Mandate:** AI is strictly advisory. It cannot execute trades, manage funds, or rebalance portfolios autonomously.
* **Mandatory Disclaimer:** Must be visible on every AI screen.
* **Audit Trail:** Every AI recommendation, along with the inputs provided to the model, the school breakdown, and the exact output, must be immutably logged and retained for **7 years**.
* **User Consent:** Users are physically gated from the AI features until they check a legally binding EULA acknowledging the risks of AI financial analysis.

### 6.2 PDPL 2020 (Personal Data Protection Law)
* **Explicit Consent:** Utilizing a user's portfolio data to personalize AI insights requires explicit, separate opt-in consent.
* **Right to Opt-Out:** Users can instantly revoke AI personalization. In this state, the AI operates in "Global Mode", analyzing the market broadly without context of the user's holdings.
* **Data Isolation:** Cross-user data contamination is physically impossible; user A's portfolio performance can never influence the prompt context for user B.

## Section 7 — AI Transparency (Explainability Standards)

The "black box" model is unacceptable in Tradeora. Every AI output must be hyper-explainable.

### 7.1 Required Output Elements
Every AI recommendation payload MUST include:
1. **Direction:** (BUY / HOLD / SELL)
2. **Confidence Level:** Displayed as a percentage (e.g., `82%`).
3. **Consensus Metric:** "9 or more of 12 Phase 1 analytical perspectives agree."
4. **Top 3 Reasons:** In Arabic and English, strictly grounded in data.
5. **FRA Disclaimer:** In legally reviewed Arabic text.
6. **Timestamp:** Exact time of analysis and data freshness marker.

### 7.2 Prohibited Language
The LLM prompt contains strict negative constraints prohibiting the following phrases and concepts:
* Guarantees of profit, return, or yield.
* Claims of certainty ("this will definitely...", "guaranteed to...").
* FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) tactics or urgency markers ("buy now before it's too late").
* Any phrasing implying Tradeora acts as a licensed human financial advisor.

## Section 11 — Hallucination Taxonomy & Mitigation

### 11.1 Hallucination Categories in Financial AI

| Category | Example | Severity | Mitigation |
|----------|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Factual hallucination** | AI claims EPS is 2.50 EGP when it is actually 1.80 EGP | CRITICAL | Cross-verify all financial facts against TimescaleDB canonical source |
| **Temporal hallucination** | AI references Q3 2026 earnings during Q1 2026 | HIGH | available_from_timestamp enforcement (Rule 40) |
| **Entity hallucination** | AI confuses COMI (CIB) with CMNT | HIGH | Ticker → ISIN canonicalization before any reasoning |
| **Confidence hallucination** | AI outputs confidence=0.95 when only 4 schools participated | CRITICAL | Safety Check 2: minimum 9 of 12 Phase 1 schools required |
| **Linguistic hallucination** | Arabic explanation contradicts the BUY recommendation | HIGH | Arabic explanation parsed against recommendation direction |
| **Regulatory hallucination** | AI says "this is a guaranteed return" | CRITICAL | Prohibited phrase list enforced via regex before output |

### 11.2 Anti-Hallucination Implementation

```python
# ai-consensus-orchestrator/src/safety/anti_hallucination.py
import re
from decimal import Decimal

# Prohibited phrases — any of these in output triggers BLOCK
PROHIBITED_PHRASES_AR = [
    "عائد مضمون",        # "guaranteed return"
    "لن ينخفض",          # "will not fall"
    "ستتضاعف",           # "will double"
    "مؤكد",              # "certain"
    "بدون خسارة",        # "without loss"
    "توصية استثمار",     # "investment recommendation" (must say "analysis")
]

PROHIBITED_PHRASES_EN = [
    "guaranteed return",
    "will not fall",
    "certain profit",
    "100% sure",
    "investment advice",  # Must say "investment analysis"
    "you should buy",     # Must say "analysis suggests"
]

class AntiHallucinationChecker:
    def check_explanation(self, explanation_ar: str, explanation_en: str) -> HallucinationResult:
        violations = []
        
        for phrase in PROHIBITED_PHRASES_AR:
            if phrase in explanation_ar:
                violations.append(HallucinationViolation(
                    type="PROHIBITED_PHRASE",
                    language="AR",
                    phrase=phrase,
                    severity="CRITICAL",
                ))
        
        for phrase in PROHIBITED_PHRASES_EN:
            if phrase.lower() in explanation_en.lower():
                violations.append(HallucinationViolation(
                    type="PROHIBITED_PHRASE",
                    language="EN",
                    phrase=phrase,
                    severity="CRITICAL",
                ))
        
        return HallucinationResult(
            has_violations=len(violations) > 0,
            violations=violations,
            action="BLOCK" if violations else "PASS",
        )
    
    def verify_financial_facts(
        self,
        explanation: str,
        market_context: MarketContext,
    ) -> FactVerificationResult:
        """
        Extract any numerical claims from the explanation and
        verify they are within 5% tolerance of actual market data.
        This catches temporal hallucinations and factual errors.
        """
        # Extract prices mentioned in explanation (regex)
        mentioned_prices = re.findall(r'(\d+\.?\d*)\s*(?:جنيه|EGP|ج\.م)', explanation)
        
        violations = []
        for price_str in mentioned_prices:
            mentioned = Decimal(price_str)
            actual = market_context.last_price
            deviation = abs(mentioned - actual) / actual
            
            if deviation > Decimal('0.05'):  # 5% tolerance
                violations.append(FactualError(
                    claimed=mentioned,
                    actual=actual,
                    deviation=deviation,
                ))
        
        return FactVerificationResult(
            violations=violations,
            action="BLOCK" if violations else "PASS",
        )
```

## Section 12 — AI Bias Audit Protocol

### 12.1 Bias Categories Monitored

| Bias Type | Description | Detection Method | Audit Frequency |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Recency bias** | Overweighting recent market events | Compare weight given to events by time period | Monthly |
| **Sector bias** | Systematically over-recommending one sector | Track BUY distribution across EGX sectors | Monthly |
| **Size bias** | Systematic preference for large-cap (EGX30) vs. small-cap | Compare recommendation rate by market cap decile | Quarterly |
| **Confirmation bias** | Schools that always agree with each other (correlated failure) | School correlation matrix analysis | Monthly |
| **Arabic explanation bias** | Explanation quality significantly worse for non-EGX30 stocks | Human evaluation sampling | Monthly |
| **Weekend return bias** | AI ignores weekend news effect (EGX trades Sun-Thu) | Back-test weekend gap performance | Quarterly |

### 12.2 Monthly Bias Audit Procedure

```python
# scripts/monthly_bias_audit.py

class MonthlyBiasAudit:
    def run(self, year: int, month: int) -> BiasAuditReport:
        recommendations = self.load_recommendations(year, month)
        
        # Check 1: Sector distribution bias
        sector_distribution = Counter(
            self.get_sector(rec.ticker) for rec in recommendations
        )
        total = len(recommendations)
        sector_bias = {
            sector: Decimal(count) / Decimal(total)
            for sector, count in sector_distribution.items()
        }
        
        # Flag: any single sector > 40% of all BUY recommendations
        sector_bias_violations = {
            sector: pct for sector, pct in sector_bias.items()
            if pct > Decimal('0.40') and sector_distribution_buy[sector] / total_buy > Decimal('0.40')
        }
        
        # Check 2: School correlation matrix
        school_votes = self.extract_school_votes(recommendations)
        correlation_matrix = self.compute_pairwise_correlation(school_votes)
        
        # Flag: school pairs with correlation > 0.90 (near-identical opinions)
        high_correlation_pairs = [
            (s1, s2, corr)
            for s1, s2, corr in correlation_matrix
            if corr > Decimal('0.90') and s1 != s2
        ]
        
        # Check 3: Recommendation direction distribution
        buy_rate = Decimal(sum(1 for r in recommendations if r.recommendation == 'BUY')) / Decimal(len(recommendations))
        sell_rate = Decimal(sum(1 for r in recommendations if r.recommendation == 'SELL')) / Decimal(len(recommendations))
        
        # Flag: If > 80% BUY in a down market month → potential bullish bias
        egx30_monthly_return = self.get_egx30_return(year, month)
        if egx30_monthly_return < Decimal('-0.05') and buy_rate > Decimal('0.80'):
            # Bullish bias in down market — flag for investigation
            pass
        
        return BiasAuditReport(
            period=f"{year}-{month:02d}",
            sector_distribution=sector_bias,
            sector_violations=sector_bias_violations,
            school_correlation_pairs=high_correlation_pairs,
            buy_rate=buy_rate,
            sell_rate=sell_rate,
            egx30_return=egx30_monthly_return,
            requires_intervention=len(sector_bias_violations) > 0 or len(high_correlation_pairs) > 0,
        )
```

### 14.1 Automatic School Exclusion Triggers

```python
# wi-wisdom-engine/src/exclusion/school_circuit_breaker.py

class SchoolCircuitBreaker:
    """
    Automatic school exclusion based on performance and health metrics.
    Excluded schools do not participate in consensus until reinstated.
    """
    
    # Exclusion conditions
    ACCURACY_MINIMUM = Decimal('0.55')           # < 55% directional accuracy
    CONSECUTIVE_MONTHS_BELOW = 3                  # 3 consecutive months below threshold
    LATENCY_MAXIMUM_MS = 1500                     # > 1500ms P99 latency
    LATENCY_SUSTAINED_MINUTES = 10                # Sustained > 10 minutes
    ERROR_RATE_MAXIMUM = Decimal('0.20')          # > 20% inference errors
    ERROR_RATE_SUSTAINED_MINUTES = 5             # Sustained > 5 minutes
    
    def evaluate_school(self, school_id: str) -> ExclusionDecision:
        accuracy_record = self.accuracy_tracker.get_monthly_accuracy(school_id, months=3)
        
        # Condition 1: Sustained low accuracy
        if all(a < self.ACCURACY_MINIMUM for a in accuracy_record[-3:]):
            return ExclusionDecision(
                should_exclude=True,
                reason="ACCURACY_BELOW_MINIMUM_3_MONTHS",
                school_id=school_id,
                reinstatement_criteria="Accuracy ≥ 65% for 2 consecutive weeks in shadow mode",
            )
        
        # Condition 2: Latency breach
        p99_latency_ms = self.metrics.get_p99_latency(school_id, window_minutes=10)
        if p99_latency_ms > self.LATENCY_MAXIMUM_MS:
            return ExclusionDecision(
                should_exclude=True,
                reason="LATENCY_BREACH",
                school_id=school_id,
                reinstatement_criteria="P99 latency < 1200ms for 5 consecutive minutes",
            )
        
        return ExclusionDecision(should_exclude=False, school_id=school_id)
    
    def reinstate_school(self, school_id: str) -> ReinstatementResult:
        """
        Schools re-enter consensus in shadow mode first.
        Shadow mode: votes counted but not weighted in final consensus.
        Full reinstatement: after 2 weeks of shadow mode with ≥ 65% accuracy.
        """
        return self.shadow_mode_manager.enter_shadow(school_id, duration_weeks=2)
```

## Section 16 — AI Ethics Board

### 16.1 Purpose

The AI Ethics Board is a permanent governance body responsible for oversight of
Tradeora's AI systems, with authority to suspend, modify, or terminate any AI
feature that poses ethical, regulatory, or financial risk to users.

### 16.2 Composition

| Role | Background | Authority |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Chief AI Officer (Chair) | AI/ML engineering | Operational decisions |
| External Financial Ethicist | Academic or NGO | Advisory + veto on ethical violations |
| FRA Liaison (observer) | Regulatory | No vote; provides regulatory context |
| User Representative | Retail investor (elected) | User interest perspective |
| Compliance Officer | Legal/compliance | Legal authority to halt AI features |

### 16.3 Standing Agenda (Quarterly)

```
1. Monthly accuracy reports review (all 3 months since last meeting)
2. Bias audit findings (sector, size, confirmation bias)
3. Hallucination incident review (Class A-D)
4. School exclusion/reinstatement decisions
5. FRA compliance status
6. User complaints related to AI recommendations
7. Planned AI model changes (approval required before production)
8. External AI ethics developments (regulatory, academic)
```

### 16.4 Ethics Board Veto Power

The Ethics Board may issue a **suspension directive** requiring immediate shutdown of
AI recommendations if:

- Hallucination rate exceeds 5% for any 7-day period
- Evidence of systematic bias disadvantaging a user segment
- FRA compliance violation affecting AI outputs
- Material AI model change deployed without Ethics Board approval

A suspension directive takes effect within 4 hours of issuance and requires a remediation
plan submitted within 48 hours before reinstatement.
