## 1. Vision

Tradeora exists to eliminate systemic opacity, information asymmetry, and technological fragmentation in modern capital markets. Traditionally, enterprise-grade financial research, real-time risk modeling, quantitative analytics, and sophisticated portfolio intelligence have been locked behind expensive institutional paywalls, legacy desktop terminals, and closed proprietary networks. Retail investors, active traders, wealth managers, and regional financial institutions in emerging and growth markets—starting with the Egyptian Exchange (EGX) and extending across the MENA region and global markets—have suffered from fragmented tools, unverified financial claims, low-precision data feeds, and opaque financial advice.

Tradeora solves this global problem by building the world's most trusted, transparent, explainable, and resilient **AI-Powered Financial Operating System**. Tradeora bridges raw market data, multi-modal financial research, predictive risk analytics, automated portfolio management, and enterprise advisory workflows into a single unified platform. 

The long-term future Tradeora will build is a global financial ecosystem where every financial decision—from individual retail investments to multi-billion-dollar institutional portfolio rebalancing—is backed by real-time mathematical precision, non-custodial software execution, auditable AI intelligence, and absolute human control. Tradeora will stand as the definitive technological standard for financial intelligence, enabling seamless, cross-border capital analysis across EGX, MENA, and global markets.

## 3. Business Boundaries

To ensure complete regulatory clarity, operational compliance, and functional focus, Tradeora strictly demarcates what it IS and what it IS NOT:

### 3.1 Tradeora IS:
1. **An AI-Powered Financial Operating System (OS):** A core software platform providing data ingestion, financial research, portfolio analytics, risk modeling, strategy synthesis, and intelligence orchestration.
2. **A Non-Custodial Financial Intelligence Engine:** Software designed to process, analyze, and visualize market data without ever holding, controlling, or taking ownership of user funds or securities.
3. **An Enterprise Advisory & Copilot Platform:** A decision-support framework that equips financial professionals and individual investors with explainable AI models, risk stress-testing, and automated research generation.
4. **An Integration & Orchestration Layer:** A high-throughput API-first platform that connects to licensed brokers, custodians, market exchanges, and third-party data providers via standardized protocols.
5. **A Multi-Market Analytics Engine:** A multi-tenant software system built specifically to handle the structural nuances, currencies, corporate actions, and regulatory frameworks of EGX, MENA, and Global asset classes.

### 3.2 Tradeora IS NOT:
1. **NOT a Trading Brokerage:** Tradeora does not hold broker-dealer licenses, maintain proprietary trading desks, internalize order flows, or act as an execution counterparty.
2. **NOT a Financial Exchange or Clearinghouse:** Tradeora does not operate matching engines for security order clearing, settlement, or central depository operations.
3. **NOT a Custodial Wallet or Asset Escrow:** Tradeora never accepts cash deposits, custody of physical or digital securities, or management of client monetary accounts.
4. **NOT an Autonomous Un-Gated Financial Advisor:** Tradeora does not execute discretionary portfolio actions or financial trades without explicit user authorization or predefined, machine-enforceable, human-governed parameter policies.
5. **NOT a Black-Box "Tip" or Gambling Platform:** Tradeora strictly rejects non-explainable financial signals, unverified speculative claims, and non-auditable prediction models.

## 4. Core Principles

Every design, architectural decision, code commit, and operational workflow across Tradeora must strictly conform to the following core principles. Each principle is an absolute mandate:

1. **AI First:** AI is embedded as a foundational intelligence layer throughout the system architecture rather than treated as an ad-hoc add-on. Every domain service, analytical pipeline, and user flow is designed from inception to consume, process, and enrich structured inputs through multi-agent AI networks, machine learning models, and cognitive analytics engines to deliver real-time predictive insights and context-aware intelligence.
2. **Human Always In Control:** Human agency remains the supreme decision-making authority across all financial workflows and order execution paths. While AI systems generate recommendations, detect anomalies, synthesize research, and calculate risk profiles, no binding financial transaction, capital allocation, or strategy modification occurs without explicit human authorization or human-predefined, machine-enforceable governance policies.
3. **Trust Above Everything:** Trust is the primary currency of Tradeora, superseding feature velocity, short-term convenience, and market hype. The platform enforces absolute data integrity, absolute transparency in financial algorithms, non-repudiable transaction logging, and verifiable calculation chains to ensure every user, advisor, and institution can depend implicitly on the system's output.
4. **Explainability:** Every AI inference, recommendation, risk metric, and analytical output produced by Tradeora must be accompanied by full causal explainability, mathematical attribution, and underlying data references. Black-box outputs are strictly forbidden; users must always be able to inspect the assumptions, historical factors, statistical confidence, and logic paths that generated any insight.
5. **Security by Design:** Security controls are architected into every structural component, API contract, and deployment pattern from day one rather than applied retroactively. Tradeora incorporates zero-trust architecture, strict network isolation, automated vulnerability scanning, mandatory code signing, and continuous posture monitoring across all software lifecycle stages.
6. **Privacy by Design:** User identity, financial holdings, trade histories, and behavioral telemetry are classified as highly sensitive assets protected by default privacy boundaries. Tradeora minimizes data collection to strictly necessary metrics, enforces end-to-end encryption for sensitive datastores, isolates tenant environments, and guarantees that personal customer data is never used to train global AI models without explicit opt-in consent.
7. **Financial Accuracy:** Precision and mathematical correctness are non-negotiable architectural mandates where rounding errors, dropped precision, or corrupted state represent catastrophic system failures. All monetary calculations, yield calculations, exposure metrics, and pricing algorithms must use arbitrary-precision fixed-point representations, deterministic financial math libraries, and strict validation against official exchange rules.
8. **Scalability:** Tradeora is engineered to scale horizontally and elastically across data processing, stream ingestion, AI inference, and user session layers to handle hyper-spikes in market volatility. Architecture boundaries isolate compute-heavy analytical pipelines from real-time quote feeds, ensuring that sudden order book volume spikes never degrade system responsiveness or compromise data freshness.
9. **Reliability:** System components are built for fault isolation, high availability, and resilient degradation under extreme operational stress. Core infrastructure guarantees high uptime for market data ingestion and portfolio evaluation through active-active redundancy, automated failover capabilities, zero-downtime rolling deployments, and self-healing cluster operations.
10. **Maintainability:** The codebase and system infrastructure are organized with strict adherence to architectural modularity, clear separation of concerns, and standardized design patterns to minimize long-term technical debt. Code readability, clear domain models, comprehensive inline technical documentation, and explicit component interfaces take precedence over clever, cryptic, or dense single-line code constructs.
11. **Observability:** Complete operational visibility into every tier of the platform is mandatory, achieved through unified logging, structured metrics collection, distributed tracing, and real-time health checks. Every business transaction, system event, AI query execution, and API request emits trace correlation identifiers to enable instantaneous root-cause analysis and proactive anomaly detection.
12. **Performance:** Low latency, high throughput, and minimal resource utilization are treated as core functional requirements across all microservices and client applications. Financial quote feeds, order status updates, and risk notifications must satisfy strict SLA targets, utilizing high-performance streaming, optimized memory layouts, and efficient serialization formats.
13. **Accessibility:** Tradeora's visual and interactive interfaces must comply fully with international WCAG accessibility standards, ensuring complete usability for individuals with visual, auditory, cognitive, or motor impairments. Keyboard navigation, screen-reader compatibility, high-contrast visual modes, and adaptive layouts are built into the UI design system as mandatory criteria.
14. **Internationalization:** System architecture natively supports multi-region operations, multi-currency accounting, localized financial formatting, and regional timezone adjustments at the core data layer. All dynamic text, date structures, financial numbers, and regulatory disclaimers are fully decoupled from UI source code through structured internationalization bundles.
15. **Arabic First-Class Support:** As a platform originating in the Egyptian Exchange (EGX) and expanding across the MENA region, Arabic is treated as an equal, first-class native language alongside English. All user experiences, financial reports, AI conversational interfaces, chart annotations, and legal disclosures support native Right-to-Left (RTL) layouts, localized Arabic terminology, and native Arabic NLP models.
16. **English First-Class Support:** English is fully supported as a primary interface and global standard language across all platform APIs, customer-facing applications, developer documentation, and AI intelligence engines. The platform maintains parity across Arabic and English experiences, allowing seamless live language switching without session disruption or data loss.
17. **Regulatory Readiness:** Software modules are structured to satisfy current and emerging regulatory frameworks governed by applicable regulatory authorities in all operating jurisdictions. Features such as regulatory reporting export, KYC/AML integration hooks, customer risk suitability profiling, and audit logging are engineered directly into the core domain logic.
18. **Modularity:** System architecture is decomposed into distinct, self-contained domain modules with clear boundaries and minimal cross-module dependencies. Each module encapsulates its business rules, data entities, and persistent storage, communicating with external domains exclusively through defined interface contracts and event schemas.
19. **Extensibility:** Tradeora is built with a pluggable architecture that enables rapid integration of new asset classes, market data providers, execution brokers, AI models, and custom user plugins without altering core engine logic. Well-defined extension points, strategy provider interfaces, and plugin hooks allow the ecosystem to grow organically.
20. **Offline Tolerance where appropriate:** Client applications and edge analytical services are designed to remain functional during temporary network disconnections, preserving local cached market views, queued user actions, and read-only portfolio analysis. When connectivity is restored, systems perform deterministic state reconciliation without data corruption or duplicate submission risks.
21. **Open Integration:** The platform exposes clean, well-documented, versioned machine-readable API contracts and streaming protocol interfaces to enable seamless integration with external wealth management systems, enterprise risk platforms, custom trading engines, and third-party data feeds. Standardized protocol contracts guarantee inter-operability across corporate IT environments.
22. **No Vendor Lock-in:** All infrastructure, database systems, messaging backbones, container runtimes, identity federation solutions, secrets management systems, and AI models must rely on open standards and vendor-neutral abstractions. Tradeora must be deployable across any cloud provider or on-premises bare metal infrastructure without requiring proprietary cloud vendor lock-in or non-portable managed APIs.
23. **Domain Driven Design:** The platform's software structure mirrors the explicit domain boundaries, ubiquitous language, and bounded contexts of enterprise finance. System abstractions directly reflect real-world financial concepts such as Order, Execution, Position, Asset, Portfolio, Ledger, Risk Engine, and Market Data Feed.
24. **Event Driven Thinking:** State changes across the Tradeora ecosystem are modeled as immutable, timestamped domain events published to a distributed message bus. Services react asynchronously to event streams (such as price ticks, order fills, risk threshold breaches, or AI insight generations), guaranteeing low coupling and high event throughput.
25. **Automation First:** Manual operational, deployment, testing, and compliance tasks are strictly minimized in favor of deterministic infrastructure-as-code pipelines, automated test suites, continuous deployment validation, and automated compliance reporting. Human intervention is reserved for governance, strategic decisions, and exceptional operational reviews.
26. **Documentation First:** No code, feature, API, or domain model may be merged into production without complete, up-to-date, and standardized technical and business documentation. Architecture decisions, API endpoints, domain event contracts, and operational runbooks must be documented as living code artifacts within the source repository.
27. **Testing First:** System reliability is guaranteed through test-driven development methodologies, comprehensive unit testing, integration testing, scenario-based simulation, and continuous end-to-end regression test suites. Critical financial calculation engines require comprehensive path test coverage and property-based testing prior to production release approval.
28. **API First:** Platform capabilities are designed and specified as clean, machine-readable API contracts before implementation code or user interfaces are constructed. API contracts serve as the canonical binding agreement between frontend clients, background microservices, AI inference endpoints, and external third-party integrations.
29. **Backward Compatibility:** Platform APIs, database schemas, message schemas, and client protocols must strictly preserve backward compatibility through clear semantic versioning rules and deprecation policies. Breaking changes are prohibited within major API versions; legacy clients must continue operating seamlessly alongside modern services.
30. **Zero Magic:** Codebases must avoid implicit framework magic, hidden reflection side-effects, automatic global state manipulations, or dynamic runtime monkey-patching. System logic must be explicit, transparent, easy to trace via static analysis, and straightforward for any engineer or automated code reviewer to comprehend immediately.
31. **Deterministic Business Logic:** Financial computations, order state transition mechanics, risk evaluation rules, and portfolio accounting functions must be strictly deterministic. Given identical inputs, parameters, and historical state, the system must produce identical mathematical results across all environments and execution runtimes.
32. **Configuration over Hardcoding:** Operational parameters, fee schedules, risk limits, market operating hours, rate limits, feature flags, and AI model routing options must be stored in centralized, version-controlled dynamic configuration stores rather than hardcoded into application binaries.
33. **Single Source of Truth:** System state, user account balances, market pricing reference tables, and security master records must maintain an authoritative, single source of truth across all domain microservices. Data duplication across database nodes must be synchronized deterministically using event streams or distributed transaction patterns.
34. **No Hidden Logic:** All fee rules, risk scoring calculations, AI weighting metrics, portfolio rebalancing models, and automated recommendations must be completely transparent to internal auditors and system administrators. Hidden fees, obscured calculations, undisclosed promotional biases, or undocumented algorithmic adjustments are strictly forbidden.
35. **Fail Fast:** Software modules must validate input data, schema contracts, authorization credentials, and invariant state pre-conditions immediately upon boundary entry. If any invariant check fails, the component must immediately reject execution, log structured context, and return explicit error payloads rather than propagating corrupted state downstream.
36. **Graceful Recovery:** When system components encounter transient network partitions, hardware failures, or dependency outages, they must isolate failure impact, execute retry strategies with exponential backoff and jitter, utilize fallback cached data where appropriate, and self-heal automatically upon system recovery.
37. **Least Privilege:** Access rights for human users, microservices, background jobs, database connections, and AI agents must be granted on a strict, need-to-know basis. Every principal is assigned only the minimum permissions required to perform its specific operational role.
38. **Defense in Depth:** Security controls must be implemented in multiple concentric layers across client applications, API gateways, network boundaries, service-to-service calls, storage backends, and physical infrastructure. No single security control or perimeter firewall may be relied upon as the sole defense mechanism.
39. **Version Everything:** Source code, database migrations, infrastructure configurations, API contracts, domain event schemas, ML models, prompt templates, and project constitution amendments must be stored under strict semantic version control with immutable tag histories.
40. **Everything Auditable:** Every user action, administrative override, system state transition, API call, database mutation, and AI inference output must generate an immutable, tamper-evident audit record containing precise timestamps, actor identity, request parameters, and correlation tokens.

## 7. Domain Language Rules

To protect the integrity of Tradeora's Domain Model and prevent technical and business miscommunication, engineering teams must enforce strict, unambiguous domain language boundaries. Inconsistent or interchangeable usage of distinct domain concepts is strictly forbidden across code repositories, API contracts, database schemas, UI text, and documentation.

### 7.1 Mandatory Domain Terminology Distinctions

1. **Trade ≠ Order:**
   * An **Order** is an explicit instruction submitted by a user or strategy requesting to buy or sell a security under specific parameters (e.g., Limit, Market). An Order may be Pending, Filled, Partially Filled, Cancelled, or Rejected.
   * A **Trade** (Execution) is an executed transaction resulting from an Order being filled by a market venue or counterparty. A single Order may yield multiple Trades.
2. **Recommendation ≠ Signal:**
   * A **Signal** is a raw, quantitative, technical, or fundamental output generated by an analytical algorithm or indicator model (e.g., RSI crossover, earnings momentum flag). A Signal carries no personal suitability context.
   * A **Recommendation** is a personalized, explainable, risk-adjusted analytical proposal synthesized by AI engines for a specific user profile and portfolio context, accompanied by confidence scores and downside risk disclosures.
3. **Portfolio ≠ Account:**
   * An **Account** represents a user's legal identity, credential profile, billing entitlement, and administrative relationship with Tradeora or an external broker.
   * A **Portfolio** is a specific, logical collection of asset holdings, cash positions, and order histories managed under defined risk parameters and benchmark constraints. An Account may manage multiple Portfolios.
4. **Asset ≠ Instrument:**
   * An **Asset** is the underlying economic entity or security class possessing intrinsic financial value (e.g., Apple Inc. Common Stock, Egyptian Treasury Bill).
   * An **Instrument** is a tradable financial contract referencing an Asset within a specific market venue, including venue-specific tickers, lot sizes, trading currency, and settlement rules.
5. **Strategy ≠ Algorithm:**
   * An **Algorithm** is a deterministic mathematical or computational sequence executing a specific calculation (e.g., Black-Scholes pricing, DCF valuation, sorting algorithm).
   * A **Strategy** is a high-level financial methodology combining quantitative algorithms, risk constraints, asset allocation rules, and rebalancing parameters to achieve a specific investment objective.
6. **Insight ≠ News:**
   * **News** is raw, unstructured information, news articles, or regulatory announcements ingested from third-party media sources.
   * An **Insight** is an enriched, contextualized analytical output produced by Tradeora's AI engines that synthesizes News, fundamental data, and market pricing into a structured, high-value notification for a specific user context.

## 8. Product Vision

Tradeora delivers a unified Financial Operating System structured across a nine-stage value creation journey:

```
[1. Data Ingestion & Security Master]
                 │
                 ▼
[2. Technical & Fundamental Analysis Engine]
                 │
                 ▼
[3. Deep Research & Automated Intelligence]
                 │
                 ▼
[4. AI Recommendations & Strategy Synthesis]
                 │
                 ▼
[5. Portfolio Intelligence & Real-Time Risk Engine]
                 │
                 ▼
[6. Execution Orchestration (Broker Integration)]
                 │
                 ▼
[7. Wealth Management & Rebalancing Engine]
                 │
                 ▼
[8. Financial Advisory & Copilot Workflows]
                 │
                 ▼
[9. Institutional & Enterprise Intelligence Suite]
```

### Stage 1: Data Ingestion & Security Master
The platform ingests real-time tick data, order books, corporate actions, financial statements, macro indicators, news feeds, and sentiment data across EGX, MENA, and global markets. The Security Master unifies disparate ticker formats, cross-exchange mappings, and asset classification standards.

### Stage 2: Analysis & Technical/Fundamental Analytics
Raw data is processed through deterministic calculation engines to compute technical indicators, corporate valuation metrics, discounted cash flow (DCF) models, dividend discount models, factor scores, and liquidity metrics with high precision.

### Stage 3: Research & Intelligence Synthesis
Multi-modal AI agents parse unstructured financial reports, earnings call transcripts, regulatory filings, and news articles (in Arabic and English) to generate structured research summaries, financial ratio attributions, and qualitative threat matrices.

### Stage 4: Recommendations & Strategy Synthesis
Explainable AI engines combine fundamental analysis, quantitative signals, and macro context to formulate personalized, risk-adjusted investment recommendations. Each recommendation includes mandatory confidence scores, explicit assumptions, underlying data points, and downside risk scenarios.

### Stage 5: Portfolio Intelligence & Risk Engine
Portfolios are continuously evaluated for real-time Value at Risk (VaR), Sharpe/Sortino ratios, sector concentrations, correlation matrices, liquidity stress, and currency risk across multi-asset holdings.

### Stage 6: Execution Orchestration (Broker Integrations)
When user-authorized, Tradeora routes standardized order parameters to licensed external brokers and execution venues via Smart Order Router (SOR) abstractions, tracking full order lifecycle states from placement to settlement.

### Stage 7: Wealth Management & Rebalancing
Automated algorithms generate tax-aware, low-slippage portfolio rebalancing plans to align portfolios with user target asset allocations, risk thresholds, and investment horizons.

### Stage 8: Financial Advisory & Copilot Workflows
Financial advisors and wealth managers utilize interactive AI Copilot interfaces to generate customized client reports, simulate scenario stress-tests, and conduct compliance suitability reviews.

### Stage 9: Enterprise Intelligence Suite
Institutions, hedge funds, and corporate treasuries utilize multi-tenant enterprise dashboards, team permissioning controls, custom model deployments, and API data streams for enterprise-wide risk governance.

## 11. AI Philosophy

AI systems within Tradeora operate under strict, immutable philosophical boundaries:

1. **Human Override Above All:** No AI model possesses autonomous, irreversible authority over capital allocations, trade orders, or portfolio modifications. Every AI output is an analytical recommendation that can be instantaneously overridden, muted, or rejected by human operators.
2. **Mandatory Explainability:** Pure black-box models are strictly prohibited in user-facing analytical flows. Every AI-generated score, summary, or prediction must expose its underlying data points, statistical weights, mathematical formulas, and causal reasoning.
3. **Explicit Confidence Scores:** AI engines must quantify their statistical uncertainty. Every recommendation or prediction payload must include a confidence score (0.00% to 100.00%) derived from model calibration, data freshness, and historical accuracy metrics.
4. **Transparent Assumptions:** AI models must explicitly declare all contextual assumptions relied upon during inference (e.g., benchmark interest rate assumptions, inflation rate projections, terminal growth rate assumptions).
5. **Mandatory Risk Disclosures:** Every AI-generated investment insight must present potential downside scenarios, maximum drawdown estimates, and risk warnings with visual prominence equal to upside opportunities.
6. **Zero Fabricated Data (Absolute Hallucination Prohibition):** AI models are strictly forbidden from fabricating financial figures, news citations, ticker symbols, historical prices, or regulatory filings. Grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with verifiable source attributions is mandatory.
7. **Complete Process Transparency:** Users must be able to inspect the prompt structures, retrieval context documents, model versions, and data pipelines that contributed to any specific AI output.
8. **Algorithmic Bias Awareness:** AI models must undergo continuous testing to detect and mitigate systemic biases toward specific market caps, geographic sectors, language models, or historical bull/bear regimes.
9. **Auditable AI Lifecycles:** Every inference query, input token payload, retrieved document context, output response, and model hyperparameter configuration must be immutably logged for audit verification.
10. **Reproducible AI Inference:** Given identical model versions, seed parameters, prompt templates, and input contexts, AI systems must produce deterministic, reproducible analytical evaluation outputs.

## 13. Engineering Principles

Tradeora's architecture is governed by ten enterprise software engineering principles:

1. **Domain-Driven Design (DDD):** Systems are modeled around explicitly defined Bounded Contexts, Aggregates, Entities, Value Objects, and Domain Events that reflect real-world financial operations. Cross-context communication occurs strictly via event schemas or formal API contracts.
2. **Clean Architecture:** Code repositories must enforce strict layer boundaries using the Dependency Inversion Rule:
   * **Core Domain Layer:** Pure business logic and domain entities (zero external framework dependencies).
   * **Use Case Layer:** Application workflows and orchestration services.
   * **Interface Adapters Layer:** Controllers, presentors, and repository implementations.
   * **Infrastructure Layer:** Databases, messaging brokers, external APIs, and UI frameworks.
3. **SOLID Principles:**
   * **Single Responsibility (SRP):** Every class or module has one, and only one, reason to change.
   * **Open/Closed (OCP):** Software entities are open for extension, but closed for modification.
   * **Liskov Substitution (LSP):** Subtypes must be completely substitutable for their base types.
   * **Interface Segregation (ISP):** Clients must not be forced to depend on interfaces they do not use.
   * **Dependency Inversion (DIP):** Depend upon abstractions, not concrete implementations.
4. **Command Query Responsibility Segregation (CQRS):** Write operations (Commands) that mutate financial state are decoupled from Read operations (Queries) that project analytical views. CQRS is enforced in high-throughput domains (such as order book processing and portfolio evaluation).
5. **Event-Driven Architecture (EDA):** Domain events (e.g., `OrderFilledEvent`, `PriceUpdatedEvent`, `RiskThresholdBreachedEvent`) serve as the primary medium for asynchronous inter-service communication via durable message brokers.
6. **Vertical Slice Architecture:** Within microservices or modular monoliths, features are organized in vertical functional slices containing their specific commands, queries, handlers, and validation rules rather than horizontal layer dumps.
7. **Pragmatic Microservices (Modular Monolith First):** System capabilities begin within a tightly governed Modular Monolith with strict module boundaries. Microservices are extracted ONLY when independent scaling, security isolation, or technology stack specialization is proven necessary.
8. **High Cohesion:** Closely related financial logic, data entities, and state transition rules are kept together within single domain boundaries.
9. **Low Coupling:** Domain modules know as little as possible about other modules, communicating exclusively via published contract interfaces and event streams.
10. **Avoid Overengineering (YAGNI & KISS):** Architectural abstractions must solve immediate, concrete domain requirements. Speculative abstractions, unused framework wrappers, and hyper-complex state machines are strictly prohibited.

## 14. Coding Principles

Every software component across Tradeora must comply with ten mandatory coding rules:

1. **Zero Duplicated Logic (DRY):** Business rules, financial calculations, and validation schemas must exist in exactly one canonical location.
2. **No God Classes or Bloated Modules:** File size, class complexity, and function lengths must strictly enforce modular focus. Large monolithic files are strictly forbidden.
3. **Strict Dependency Injection:** All component dependencies must be explicitly injected via constructors. Global state, static service locators, and singletons with mutable state are banned.
4. **Strict Typing:** Dynamically typed dynamic variables (`any`, untyped dictionaries) are forbidden. All code must enforce static typing, strict null checks, and explicit interface definitions.
5. **Pure Business Rules:** Core domain entities and calculation functions must be pure functions free from side-effects, database reads, network calls, or system clock calls.
6. **No Hidden Dependencies:** System clock readings, random number generation, environment variable lookups, and file system reads must be injected behind abstract interface adapters.
7. **Domain-Accurate Naming:** Variable, function, and class names must strictly reflect financial domain language and conform to Domain Language Rules (Section 7).
8. **No Magic Numbers or Strings:** All numerical constants, status strings, configuration flags, and error codes must be defined as explicit immutable constants or typed enums.
9. **Deterministic Financial Math:** Floating-point primitive types (`float`, `double`) are strictly banned for monetary, position, price, or percentage calculations. All math must use arbitrary-precision fixed-point decimal types.
10. **Small, Focused Modules:** Every source file must fulfill a single, well-defined operational responsibility.

9. **Cryptographic Auditability:** Critical financial ledgers must generate cryptographic verification chains to guarantee data has not been tampered with at rest.

## 19. Security Principles

Tradeora implements an enterprise Zero-Trust Security Architecture structured across eight vendor-neutral principles:


3. **End-to-End Encryption:** All data in transit must enforce TLS 1.3 or higher encryption. All data at rest across databases, cache clusters, object storage, and backups must be encrypted using strong encryption keys managed via an Enterprise Key Management Solution.
4. **Enterprise Secrets Management Solution:** Plaintext passwords, API keys, private certificates, and database credentials must never be committed to source control or baked into container images. Vendor implementations must remain pluggable.

7. **Tamper-Evident Audit Logging:** Security audit logs are captured asynchronously, formatted as structured JSON, and streamed to write-once-read-many (WORM) immutable log storage for forensic analysis.
8. **Defense in Depth:** Security controls are deployed across multiple concentric layers: WAF -> API Gateway Rate Limiting -> Microservice Entitlements -> Core Domain Validation -> Database Level Encryption.

9. **NO Production Access without Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Administrative, SSH, database, or cloud console access without hardware-backed MFA is strictly prohibited.
10. **NO Un-Encrypted Financial Data at Rest or in Transit:** All datastores must enforce strong encryption at rest. All network calls must enforce TLS 1.3 or higher encryption in transit.
11. **NO Direct Cross-Bounded-Context Database Joins:** Domain microservices must NEVER join database tables across bounded context boundaries. Cross-domain queries must utilize published APIs or event streams.
12. **NO Fabrication of Financial Facts (Hallucination Prohibition):** AI agents that generate unverified financial figures, fake citations, or un-grounded ticker references must be taken offline immediately.
13. **NO Modification of Immutable Historical Ledgers:** Deleting or altering historical execution logs, transaction ledgers, or tick data is physically prohibited. Corrections require compensating offset entries.
14. **NO Code Deployment without Code Review:** No commit or PR may be merged to production branches without passing automated CI quality gates and receiving approval from at least two senior human reviewers.
15. **NO Production Deployments with Failed Tests:** Bypassing, disabling, or ignoring failing automated unit, integration, or performance tests to meet deadlines is strictly forbidden.
16. **NO Untyped Variables or Implicit Conversions:** Dynamic types (`any`, generic object pointers) are forbidden in core domain logic. Strict static typing must be enforced.
17. **NO System Clock Dependencies in Core Domain:** Direct calls to system wall-clock time inside pure domain rules are banned. Time providers must be explicitly injected interface abstractions.
18. **NO Single Point of Failure (SPOF):** Critical production services, databases, message brokers, and network paths must be deployed in active-active high-availability multi-zone configurations.
19. **NO Proprietary Cloud Vendor Lock-In Dependencies:** Application code must not bind directly to vendor-proprietary APIs. Infrastructure and platform services must rely on vendor-neutral abstractions.
20. **NO PII or Un-Scrubbed Financial Data in Application Logs:** Log streams must pass through automated sanitization filters to scrub passwords, tokens, client PII, and financial identifiers prior to output.
21. **NO Bypass of Web Application Firewall (WAF) or Rate Limiters:** Disabling security WAF rules, API rate limiters, or DDoS protection layers in production environments is strictly banned.
22. **NO Un-Audited Administrative Overrides:** System administrators and engineers cannot perform data updates, system parameter adjustments, or configuration overrides without generating immutable audit log records.

## 30. Enterprise Glossary

To ensure absolute ubiquitous language across business, engineering, and AI contexts, the following domain terms are formally defined:

1. **Asset:** Any financial instrument, security, currency, contract, or holding that possesses economic value (e.g., Stock, ETF, Bond, Option, Crypto Token).
2. **Security Master:** The single canonical registry holding authoritative reference metadata, exchange mappings, ticker mappings, and corporate action histories for all supported assets.
3. **Market:** A specific trading venue, exchange, or liquidity pool with defined operating hours, currency, regulatory rules, and order execution constraints (e.g., EGX, Tadawul, NASDAQ).
4. **Portfolio:** A structured collection of asset holdings, cash balances, and pending orders owned by a specific user, client entity, or fund.
5. **Position:** The total net quantity and calculated cost basis of a specific security asset held within a portfolio.
6. **Trade (Execution):** An executed transaction representing the purchase or sale of a specific asset quantity at an agreed price point.
7. **Order:** An explicit instruction submitted by a user or automated strategy to buy or sell a specified quantity of an asset under defined execution rules (e.g., Limit Order, Market Order, Stop-Loss Order).
8. **Signal:** A quantitative, technical, or fundamental indicator output suggesting a potential market trend, valuation discrepancy, or trade setup.
9. **Strategy:** A structured, rule-based algorithmic model that generates market signals, portfolio allocation proposals, or rebalancing workflows based on incoming data streams.
10. **Risk:** The statistical probability and potential magnitude of monetary loss or unexpected divergence from expected portfolio returns.
11. **Confidence Score:** A calibrated statistical probability metric (0.00% to 100.00%) accompanying AI inferences, expressing model certainty based on input data freshness, historical model accuracy, and context completeness.
12. **Recommendation:** An explainable, personalized, risk-adjusted analytical proposal generated by Tradeora's AI engines for human review and authorization.
13. **Research Summary:** A multi-modal, synthesized analytical document parsing news, earnings filings, financial statements, and market sentiment into structured insights.
14. **Backtest:** A historical simulation executing a quantitative strategy against past tick and corporate action data to measure risk-adjusted historical performance metrics.
15. **Execution Orchestration:** The technological framework that routes user-authorized order parameters to external licensed execution brokers and tracks full order state lifecycles.
16. **Exposure:** The total monetary value or percentage ratio of a portfolio vulnerable to specific market, asset, sector, currency, or geographical risk factors.
17. **Watchlist:** A custom collection of security assets tracked continuously by a user or AI agent for real-time price updates, news alerts, and signal generation.
18. **Insight:** A real-time, context-aware notification highlighting an anomalous market event, earnings release surprise, portfolio risk threshold breach, or strategic valuation opportunity.
19. **Smart Order Router (SOR):** An algorithmic orchestration service that analyzes liquidity, pricing, and execution fees across multiple market venues to route order parameters for optimal fill quality.
20. **Value at Risk (VaR):** A statistical risk metric calculating the maximum expected monetary loss of a portfolio over a defined time horizon at a specific confidence level.
21. **Rebalancing:** The process of executing trades to adjust portfolio asset weights back to target asset allocation limits or risk tolerance boundaries.
22. **Corporate Action:** An event initiated by a public company that impacts its equity or debt securities (e.g., Stock Split, Cash Dividend, Rights Issue, Merger, Spin-off).
23. **Drawdown:** The peak-to-trough percentage decline in portfolio value during a specific continuous time window.
24. **Bounded Context:** An explicit boundary defined by Domain-Driven Design within which a specific domain model applies consistently.
25. **Multi-Tenant Isolation:** Architectural partitioning guaranteeing that computing resources, datastores, caches, and AI model contexts of distinct corporate or institutional clients remain strictly segregated and inaccessible to other tenants.
